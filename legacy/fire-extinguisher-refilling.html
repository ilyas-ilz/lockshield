Keep every extinguisher charged, tested and certified

A fire extinguisher is only useful if it is fully charged and in date. We refill, recharge and service all types of portable fire extinguishers — dry powder (ABC), carbon dioxide (CO2), foam (AFFF) and water — for offices, warehouses, retail units, restaurants, vehicles and residential buildings across the UAE.

Every unit is inspected, discharged, refilled with the correct extinguishing agent, hydrostatically pressure-tested where due, resealed and re-certified. We supply replacement units where an extinguisher is beyond service life, and can set up scheduled refilling so nothing lapses — with fast turnaround and collection and delivery available.

What we cover

  • Dry powder (ABC), CO2, foam & water extinguisher refilling
  • Hydrostatic pressure testing & valve servicing
  • Civil Defence-compliant re-certification & tagging
  • Fast turnaround with collection & delivery available

How to get DCD Approval for Restaurants and other Fit-Outs in UAE

30 Apr 2024

Getting DCD approval is necessary to ensure the safety of your restaurant or other fit-out project in the UAE, specifically Dubai. As the well distinguished Apollo fire alarm system supplier in UAE, Lockshield Cart is here to share a general overview of the process:

Prepare Documents and Drawings

You’ll need detailed plans outlining your fit-out design, including safety features and adherence to building codes. Compile other required documents, like the contractor’s trade license and a DCD fire code invoice. LOCK SHIELD FIRE FIGHTING & SAFETY EQUIPMENT INSTALLATIONLLC helps you to prepare design as per civil defense requirements. 

Submit for Approval

The application for DCD approval typically needs to be typed in Arabic. One of the foundational steps in the DCD fit-out approval process is the submission of comprehensive fit-out drawings, including fire and safety plans, as well as other relevant drawings. These drawings serve as a visual representation of the proposed fit-out design and are crucial for demonstrating compliance with fire safety regulations and other DCD  requirements. You may need an approved company like LOCK SHIELD FIRE FIGHTING & SAFETY EQUIPMENT INSTALLATION LLC’s signature before submitting for the approval. 

Contractor Registration with DCD

Contractors undertaking fit-out projects in Dubai must be registered with the Dubai Civil Defense. This registration ensures that contractors have met the necessary qualifications and standards set by DCD, including expertise in fire safety measures and compliance with regulatory requirements. Partnering with a registered fit-out and fire alarm system contractor is a prerequisite for initiating fit-out work in Dubai. 

Work completion as per the approval

Completing your project according to DCD (Dubai Civil Defence)-approved products is crucial for obtaining final fire safety clearance and ensuring occupant safety. LOCK SHIELD FIRE FIGHTING & SAFETY EQUIPMENT INSTALLATION LLC provides civil defense-approved products for all your needs. 

Application for Initial Approval via DCD Portal

Businesses seeking to commence fit-out work must apply for initial approval through the DCD portal. This step involves submitting all required documentation and information related to the proposed fit-out project. The DCD reviews the application to ensure that the proposed design meets fire safety standards and other regulatory requirements before granting initial approval to commence work. 

Application for Final Completion from DCD

For businesses operating in the food and beverage sector, obtaining DCD approval is not just a recommendation but a mandatory requirement. Every restaurant in Dubai must have DCD approval, ensuring that specific criteria related to fire safety or fire alarm products and other regulatory standards are met. This requirement underscores the importance of prioritizing safety and compliance in restaurant fit-out projects in Dubai.

Inspections

Once submitted, DCD will likely conduct inspections to verify that your plans comply with safety regulations. Civil defense cardholders are to be present along with the inspector for checking.

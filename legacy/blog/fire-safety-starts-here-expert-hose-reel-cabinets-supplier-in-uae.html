Proper Placement and Regular Inspection

Proper cabinet placement within the building is just as important as the quality of the fire hose reel cabinet itself. They must be obvious and in prominent positions, e.g., escape routes, corridors, stairwells, and so forth, to be able to access the cabinet without any problems during a fire emergency. Regular inspections will help to ensure that the cabinets are ready to use. Inspections should check for rust, cabinet damage, and obstructions or faults in cabinet doors. The hoses must be properly coiled and connected, with no leaking water or misaligned connections to the water supply. Labels must be clearly readable. Conducting regular inspections will enable you to identify small issues before they become significant fire safety concerns.

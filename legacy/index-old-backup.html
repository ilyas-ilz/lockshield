?What is annual maintenance contract or AMC ?

An Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) in fire safety refers to a contractual agreement between a fire safety service provider and a client or organization. It outlines the terms and conditions for the regular maintenance, inspection, and servicing of fire safety equipment and systems within a specified period. As per the Dubai civil defense it should be conducted In every three month period .

?What is PPM ?

PPM stands for Planned Preventive Maintenance. It is a proactive approach to maintenance management that involves scheduled inspections, maintenance activities, and servicing of equipment and systems to prevent or minimize the occurrence of failures or breakdowns.

?What is DCD certification for fire system products

The Dubai Civil Defense (DCD) certification is specifically related to fire safety products and systems. It is required for certain products, such as fire extinguishers, fire alarms, fire suppression systems, fire-resistant materials, and other firefighting equipment, to ensure their compliance with the specified standards and regulations set by the DCD.

?How often should fire safety systems be inspected?

As per Dubai Civil Defence regulations, fire alarm and firefighting systems should be inspected and serviced at least once every three months under a valid AMC.

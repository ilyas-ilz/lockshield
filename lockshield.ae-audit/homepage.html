An AMC is a mandatory yearly service agreement in the UAE under which certified technicians inspect, test, calibrate, and service your fire fighting, alarm, and suppression systems periodically to maintain full Dubai Civil Defence (DCD) compliance.

UAE Civil Defence regulations require fire alarm and fire fighting systems to be thoroughly inspected and certified quarterly (every 3 months), along with 24/7 emergency repair coverage.

Extinguishers are checked at every scheduled inspection and must be refilled after any use or when the gauge leaves the green zone. Cylinders also need hydrostatic pressure testing every 3-5 years per Civil Defence code. We test, refill, and certify CO2, DCP, foam, and water extinguishers.

Frequently Asked Questions

  1. How often do you change the batteries for the fire alarm system?

It is recommended that fire alarm batteries be replaced every 1 – 3 years depending on frequency of usage and by the range of fire alarm manufacturers. To determine how frequently to replace the fire alarm system’s battery, fire alarm systems should be tested for battery replacement on a regular basis.

  1. Will a weak battery affect how your fire alarm works?

Yes, any time a battery has lost its charge, it may create a delay in sounding the alarm or result in false alarms if the batteries are weak, especially when electricity is not available, which could endanger you and others.

  1. Are the fire alarm batteries designed to work in weather conditions found in the UAE?

High-quality fire alarms will work correctly in warm weather. A properly qualified fire alarm battery supplier will assist with making sure that the supplier provides fire alarms that will work well in high heat.

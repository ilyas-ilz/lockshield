Why Commercial Kitchens Need Dedicated Fire Protection

Kitchen fires are unlike those occurring in most other types of buildings since they are fueled by oils and typically burn at much higher temperatures than other flammable materials. For this reason, water alone is not effective in extinguishing grease fires, and standard fire extinguishers are usually inadequate for kitchens. That’s why we offer kitchen fire prevention systems that are specifically designed to extinguish fires caused by oil and grease safely and effectively. Kitchen suppression system products detect the presence of a fire early and allow for quick action, like activating the system and delivering a fire suppression agent to cool the flames and prevent re-ignition. This rapid response can limit damage and increase staff and customer safety and also help business owners avoid costly downtime and repairs. Therefore, we always suggest that kitchen fire prevention systems be in place so that they can be used during a fire, both to save monetary dollars and lives.

FAQs

  1. What is the purpose of kitchen suppression system products?

Kitchen suppression systems are intended to remove the chance of occurrence for a fire due to grease or cooking oils caused by the heat when cooking food on the stove in a commercial kitchen. They come with an automatic fire detection system that activates automatically to dispense an agent that will extinguish a fire and prevent it from re-igniting.

Do I Require a Kitchen Suppression System in UAE?

The majority of commercial kitchens need to have an approved kitchen suppression system or a different approved kind of fire protection with regard to what the local fire safety authority requires to conform to the local laws regarding what is considered to be safe through the use of fire. Acquiring certified kitchen suppression system products will ensure that your business passes fire inspections and follows the local laws concerning fire safety compliance.

  1.           How often should you service a kitchen suppression system?

Regularly scheduled inspection and service are critical to maintaining the proper level of performance for kitchen suppression system products. Keep them properly maintained to ensure that they will be available and operate properly when there is a fire hazard.

The Significance of CO₂ Fire Extinguishers

The way CO₂ fire extinguishers work is that the oxygen supply to the fire is taken away, thus putting out the fire instantly. This extinguisher can be best applied in the case of electrical fires and flammable liquids. This means that water extinguishers cannot be used, which is quite good news for firms and organizations in the UAE, which depend on electricity.

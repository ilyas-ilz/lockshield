FAQs

  1.       Which types of fire pumps are appropriate?

The three most commonly found types of fire pumps are electric fire pumps, diesel fire pumps, and jockey pumps. The proper type of fire pump is based on the electrical power supply, the size of the fire pump, and the need to ensure fire safety.

  1. How often should I inspect or service my fire pump?

Fire pumps should be inspected weekly or at intervals of one month, and they should only be maintained by certified personnel and in compliance with the manufacturer’s recommendations, which may also involve quarterly or yearly inspection, depending on how frequently the pump is being used, as well as relevant legislation relating to fire protection equipment.

  1. Are fire pumps required in every building?

Not every building requires a fire pump. Most buildings that require a fire pump are commercial or industrial, and the only residential structures that typically require a fire pump are those that are more than one story.

  1. What can happen if my fire pump is not properly maintained?

If your fire pump is not properly maintained, it may be inoperable at the time of a fire, not supply adequate water pressure to fight the fire, or take longer to start up than expected, resulting in additional damage and possible endangerment of lives.

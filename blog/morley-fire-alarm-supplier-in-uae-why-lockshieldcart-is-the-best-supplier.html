An Insight into the Fire Alarm System

Fire alarm systems have been designed to initiate the detection of fire or smoke so as to alert people to evacuate in good time. The period taken before the people evacuate the buildings is a critical element in preventing the loss of human life out of the inferno since a fire can spread at a very high speed. In high-risk environments such as Dubai’s skyscrapers and commercial complexes, an effective fire alarm system is not only a regulatory requirement but also a necessity for ensuring safety in those areas.
